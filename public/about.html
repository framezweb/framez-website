ABOUT THE STUDIO

Back behind the lens, with more to say.

Framez started as a way to shoot the events that actually mattered — weddings, gigs, launches — the kind of days people only get once. After a short break, the studio is back and taking on new clients.

The approach hasn't changed: show up early, stay out of the way, and bring back a set of images and footage that feels like being there again.
